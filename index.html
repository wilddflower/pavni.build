about

Hi, I’m Pavni, a second-year Business + Computer Science student at UBC.

Growing up, I never related to the idea of “work-life balance,” where work drains you and life is what recharges you. I’ve always wanted a career that’s the reason I get up in the morning, excited to build.

Right now, I’m figuring out what that is, whether it’s in tech, building startups and communities, or exploring new fields altogether. The best way I’ve found is to put myself in rooms full of ambitious, driven people.

In the future, I want to build products that aren’t just functional, but create experiences people genuinely enjoy and look forward to using.

Thanks for visiting my site. If any of this resonates, let’s connect!
